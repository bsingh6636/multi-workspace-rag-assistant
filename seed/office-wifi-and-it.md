# Office Wi-Fi and IT help

## Wi-Fi

Staff use the "Office-Staff" network and sign in with their work account. Guests use "Office-Guest"; reception hands out a daily guest password.

## Laptops

New starters get a laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if they fail.

## Password resets

Reset your work password from the self-service portal. If you're locked out, the IT desk can reset it after checking your employee ID.

## Getting help

Raise an IT ticket for anything that isn't urgent. For urgent problems during office hours, visit the IT desk on the second floor.
