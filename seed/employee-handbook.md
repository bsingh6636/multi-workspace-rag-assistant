# Employee handbook

## Leave policy

Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. Unused leave carries over up to 10 days. Sick leave is separate and needs a note after three days in a row.

## Expenses

Travel and equipment expenses are claimed in the expenses tool within 30 days. Meals on work trips are covered up to a daily limit set by the finance team.

## Office

The office is open from 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday. Visitors sign in at reception and wear a badge.

## Working from home

Employees may work from home up to three days a week after their first month, with their manager's agreement.
